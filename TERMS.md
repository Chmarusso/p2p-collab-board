_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

These terms cover the OpenCollab desktop, iOS, Android and command-line apps, and any
always-on peer that the OpenCollab maintainer runs to keep boards available ("our
peers"). By using OpenCollab you agree to them.

## What OpenCollab is

OpenCollab is free, open-source software under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). It lets a team
share boards directly between their devices. There is no OpenCollab account and no server
that holds your boards: you and your team run the software and hold the data.

## Acceptable use

Don't use OpenCollab to:

- store or share anything that is illegal where you or your board's members are, including
  material that sexually exploits children, terrorist content, malware, or content that
  infringes someone else's rights;
- harass, threaten or impersonate people, or share someone's private information without
  their consent;
- attack, overload or disrupt the peer-to-peer network, our peers or other users' devices.

Board owners and admins can remove members, and every member can block or report another
member in the app.

## Our peers

The maintainer may run always-on peers that keep encrypted copies of boards online so
members can sync while others are offline. These peers can't read board content. They are
offered as is, without any guarantee of availability, and may stop serving any board at any
time, in particular after a credible report that it breaks these terms or the law.

## Reporting abuse

To report a board or member that breaks these terms, email **CONTACT_EMAIL**. Say what
happened and include the member's key, which the app shows when you report them. Don't send
illegal material or private board content. If something puts someone in immediate danger,
contact the police first.

We can't read boards or remove content from members' devices. What we can do is stop our
peers from serving a board and cooperate with lawful requests from the authorities.
